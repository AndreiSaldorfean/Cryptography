Usage: a51 [options] [message|path/to/file]

a51 is a command line tool that will encrypt a message, or the contents of a file, that is passed as argument to it.The result will be printed the result on the terminal.

Options:
  -h, --help      Display this help message.
  -v, --version   Returns the version of the software.
  -V, --verbose   Increases the verbosity of the returned output.

Exmaples:
  a51 -V "Lorem Ipsum"
  a51 --help
  a51 path/to/some/file
